Abstract

Abstract

Jigesh Sheoran is a cybersecurity student and researcher at the University of Petroleum and Energy Studies (UPES), Dehradun, graduating in 2027. His work focuses on adversarial tooling, red and purple team operations, threat hunting, and the application of machine learning to phishing detection. This document presents his research contributions, published tools, learning notes, and professional journey.
